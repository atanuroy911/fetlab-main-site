9. Dhaka Traffic Digital Twins — DTDT
This is an excellent example of a problem-driven research program.
Dhaka Traffic Digital Twins (DTDT)
Research orientation:
 Mathematical modelling · Computational modelling · Simulation · Algorithms · Digital Twins · Complex systems
Problem
Dhaka's traffic system has characteristics that make it particularly interesting as a mathematical and computational model problem.
Unlike highly structured traffic systems in cities such as Singapore, New York, or Delhi, significant portions of Dhaka's traffic dynamics involve:
negotiation;
informal consensus;
adaptation;
intuitive behaviour;
interactions among heterogeneous road users.
Research opportunity
A simplified road environment can be represented as a two-dimensional computational space in which vehicles and other road users are modelled as interacting entities.
This creates opportunities for research into:
mathematical modelling;
multi-agent systems;
simulation;
computational geometry;
optimization;
algorithmic traffic analysis;
many-body systems;
two-body interaction models;
digital twins.
A possible initial experimental environment could be a digital 2D representation of a limited road segment such as the Beribadh road area near ULAB.
Group Leader
Dr. Ashraful Kadir
Members
Hossain Al Nayem — Student Researcher, ULAB
Jumana Muntaha — Student Researcher, ULAB
Munshi Nahiyan Amin — Student Researcher, ULAB
Why DTDT matters to FETLAB
This is precisely the kind of initiative that demonstrates the FETLAB model:
A real-world problem → mathematical abstraction → multidisciplinary research → computational simulation → potential technological application.
I would make this kind of example highly visible on the eventual website.

11. Research Group Taxonomy
With these examples, I think we should not put every FETLAB group into a single flat "Research Areas" list.
There are at least three different types of entities emerging:
A. Research Groups
Focused around a sustained research theme.
Examples:
Tangible Reality / IoT / Smart Textiles
Explainable AI / Computational Epidemiology / Healthcare
Massive- & Large-scale Agentic AI
B. Research / Innovation Initiatives
Focused around a particular problem or ecosystem function.
Examples:
ResearchConnect
Dhaka Traffic Digital Twins
C. Forums
Communities bringing people together around an activity.
Example:
FETLAB Inventors & Innovators
FETLAB Student Researchers
This distinction is very valuable for the website architecture.

One particularly important change
I think "People & Network" should become one of the central pillars of FETLAB.org, rather than just a standard academic "People" page.
The organizational information you've just provided demonstrates why.
FETLAB is simultaneously:
an institution + a research network + a set of research groups + an innovation community + a student research forum + a collaboration platform.
That is considerably more distinctive than a conventional lab.
And I would make the research-group pages dynamic: each group should have its own description, leaders, members, research themes, projects, publications, and updates, so that the site can grow naturally as FETLAB evolves.
